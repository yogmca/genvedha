## 📈 KEYWORD MATCH TYPES STRATEGY

### Exact Match [keyword]
**Use for:** High-intent commercial keywords
**Benefit:** Highest conversion rate, most control
**Example:** `[custom software development company]`

### Phrase Match "keyword"
**Use for:** Core service keywords
**Benefit:** Balance of reach and relevance
**Example:** `"mobile app development"`

### Broad Match Modified +keyword
**Use for:** Discovery and testing
**Benefit:** Wider reach, find new opportunities
**Example:** `+ai +development +services`

### Negative Keywords (Critical!)
```
- free
- cheap
- diy
- tutorial
- course
- learn
- jobs
- career
- salary
- resume
- template
- download
- open source
- github
```

1. **Click-Through Rate (CTR):** Target 3-5%

## 💡 PRO TIPS FOR SUCCESS

1. **Start Small, Scale Fast:** Begin with 20-30 high-intent keywords, then expand
2. **Quality Score Matters:** Focus on relevance (ad copy + landing page + keywords)
3. **Mobile-First:** 60%+ of searches are mobile, optimize accordingly
4. **Use Ad Extensions:** Sitelinks, callouts, structured snippets
5. **Remarketing is Gold:** Target visitors who didn't convert
6. **Test Everything:** Ad copy, landing pages, CTAs, offers
7. **Track Phone Calls:** Many B2B leads call directly
8. **Competitor Targeting:** Bid on competitor brand names (carefully)
9. **Seasonal Adjustments:** Increase budget during Q4 and budget planning seasons
10. **LinkedIn Integration:** Retarget Google Ads visitors on LinkedIn
